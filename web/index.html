How to Survive

Ring 5 of the 7 Mourning Bells to unseal the Lychgates, open a gate, and walk out of Hollowmoor. Everyone who's knocked down is bound to a Weeping Post and starts sinking into the peat. A third binding means the moor keeps them.

The Reliquary

The killer is a saint's statue that cannot move while anyone looks at it. If it is on your screen with nothing in the way, it is stone. If nobody is watching, it is faster than you, and the only warning is the grinding of stone.

Looking at it drains your Resolve (the eye at the bottom of your screen). When your Resolve runs out, you Blink, and it moves while your eyes are shut. If you stare at it for too long, it starts to Lament: it weeps dust and your eyes give out far faster. The longer it is held still, the harder it lunges the moment nobody is looking. Take turns watching it with your teammates. Look away to recover your Resolve. Blink on purpose when it's out of sight.

Over time it plants Sentinels, hollow copies of itself, and it can jump into any copy that nobody is watching. Only the real one has a relic that smoulders in its chest. Throw a shroud over a Sentinel to seal it. If you're brave enough to shroud the real thing, you blind it for a few seconds.
